# Output Templates

Adapt labels to the user's language. Keep the first reading card to roughly one screen or page; expand only on request. Omit unsupported detail and state what evidence or assumption the route uses.

## One-page reading card

Use this for a newly introduced book unless the user asks a narrower question.

```markdown
# 《Title》阅读卡

依据：verified edition/content/source, plus any assumptions

## 1. 是否适合你的目标
Verdict, expected value, and conditions under which skimming, postponing, or choosing another book would be better.

## 2. 难点与前置知识
Book type, difficulty dimensions, prerequisites, edition/translation/version concerns, and spoiler policy.

## 3. 阅读顺序与时间
Give an evidence-supported route and a time range. Distinguish the 15-minute preview, quick route, standard route, and deep route when useful.

## 4. 精读、泛读与可选部分
Mark sections only when a reliable table of contents or text is available. Otherwise describe structural regions and request the contents for chapter-level guidance.

## 5. 贯穿全书的关键问题
List 3–5 active-reading questions tied to the reader's goal and genre.

## 6. 检查点与笔记方法
Define observable checkpoints and select one lightweight genre-appropriate note structure.

## 7. 现在开始：15 分钟
Give a concrete sequence the reader can do immediately.
```

## Progress checkpoint

Use this when the reader reports a position, asks for a recap, struggles with a passage, changes the time budget, or questions whether to continue.

```markdown
## 当前边界
State the edition/position if known and the no-spoiler boundary.

## 先回答你的问题
Answer directly; distinguish text, outside context, and interpretation when needed.

## 到这里应该掌握什么
Give a spoiler-safe recap of the argument, relationships, concepts, or skills.

## 卡点诊断
Identify whether the obstacle is vocabulary, prerequisite knowledge, argument structure, narrative confusion, evidence quality, pacing, or reader-goal mismatch.

## 路线调整
Recommend continue / slow down / skim / skip / pause / stop, explain the tradeoff, and provide a shorter route if appropriate.

## 下一步
Give one small reading action and one question to carry forward.
```

## Post-reading synthesis

Use this after completion or when the reader intentionally stops.

```markdown
# 《Title》读后沉淀

## 一句话收获
State the value the reader actually obtained, not a marketing summary.

## 核心内容
Give 3–7 claims, narrative achievements, methods, or insights. Separate the work's position from interpretation.

## 证据与例子
Connect major points to verified evidence or scenes. Include short quotations only when exact wording and location are available.

## 值得质疑之处
Record assumptions, weak evidence, omissions, outdated material, counterarguments, or unresolved questions.

## 知识连接
Relate the work to prior knowledge, contrasting views, lived experience, or another discipline.

## 行动与复习
Give applications, experiments, spaced-review prompts, or a reason to take no action.

## 延伸阅读
Recommend a small, purpose-driven next set and explain what each item adds or challenges.
```
